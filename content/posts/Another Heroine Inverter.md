We were planning a surprise release this week, but since Futami and Minami finally greenlit the release of the final Ancient Apostles DLC this week, we've had a change of plans. Good thing I didn't specify what the surprise was going to be, so now we can pretend that this was the intended surprise, right?  
  
As mentioned all the way back in November, the Limited Edition of Lycoris in Japan came with multiple bonuses, one of which was an original Drama CD story. What's more, due to popular demand, the producers also commisioned a visual novel rendition of that very Drama CD and included it as another bonus for the Lycoris limited edition.  
  
This Drama CD focuses on the original gameverse heroines + Yui in GGO in the sidelines while the main gang is busy with the Clamp incident in Underworld. Since it's basically a prologue for the gameverse heroines' arrival to Underworld in the aftermath of the Clamp incident, we decided to hold out on releasing this Drama CD until it become relevant... and since Strea and Philia were added to the game just yesterday, the time has finally come!  
  
I'll be posting Kaantantr's subtitled VN of the Drama CD, and the text version of the translated script. My translation was based on the Japanese script that came with the Drama CD, so you'll notice some differences between the text script and the video version.  
  
Anyway, I hope you enjoy the release. If you have any suggestions/requests for what to work on, feel free to leave a comment or contact us through Twitter/Discord/Email.  
  
Any feedback is appreciated.
