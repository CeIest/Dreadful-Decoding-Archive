**Translation (choice) comments:**  
General:  

*   For some reason, all instances of "glass" are written in kanji with furigana. Don't know why.
*   Aside from instances where actual system commands are recited, Sacred Arts elements are almost always referred to with Japanese expressions (kanji), rather than English words. Since the Japanese kanji terms aren't as sophisticated as the English variants, I translated the element names literally when they are mentioned in actual Japanese. For example, the "cryogenic" element is called the "freezing element" (凍素) outside of commands.
*   I went with Tap's translation of Scheta/Sheyta's name, because I feel that it represents the pronunciation more accurately.
*   Nearly all instances of the word "dragon" use the word 飛竜 (hiryuu, "flying dragon") in the original text. I omitted the "flying" part because a non-flying dragon would just be a lizard or something in the West. And the anime sure portrays the UW dragons as regular Westernish dragons, rather than a wyvern or something, so I'm not sure what the "flying" bit is supposed to achieve. However, Kirito does use the English words "wyvern" and "dragon" in certain instances; these instances are indicated in itallics like all katakanised English.
*   When speaking to Alice, Kirito's tone varies widely. At first he tries to sound formal by using the polite tone (丁寧語, teineigo), which is basically just using -masu and "desu" forms. However, over time, he just slips back into his usual casual tone without all the politeness markers. This even becomes the topic of the conversation at one point... and leads to him using both speech styles in the same dialogue line. This trait of his irks her, until she just gives up on him entirely. Since I can't just leave in all the "desu"s and "masu"s and call it a day, I tried making Kirito's speech sound more polite by removing any contractions and going for more formal synonyms when he's trying to be polite. You'll even find an instance or two of "ma'am" where Kirito switched styles mid-sentence and I had to find a way to account for that. Meanwhile, in the casual tone instances, I used contractions and simpler words to account for the speech style.
*   Alice usually speaks in the polite tone (teineigo) with everyone. But when speaking to Bercouli or Quinella, she ups her game by going into humble tone (謙譲語, kenjougo), an even more polite tone where she starts using extra polite synonyms and grammatical forms for a lot of words (e.g., using おる (oru) for ある (aru, "to be"); 伺う (ukagau) for 聞く (kiku "ask") and its derivative forms; adding になる (ni naru) to verb stems to make verbs sound extra formal).
*   With Quinella, Kirito's speech fluctuates from purely casual, to tacking on a polite copula ("desu") at the very end as an afterthought, to proper polite tone. In some sentences, he starts mixing proper polite tone with his pseudo-polite tone, and even casual tone with colloquialisms. To account for this, I added "ma'am"s at the end of the sentence to indicate that he's trying to at least pretend to be polite (but usually only as an afterthought), I used slang and contractions to indicate a casual tone, and avoided contractions + used more formal words to indicate an actual polite tone.
*   I decided to omit/localise honorifics used with titles (e.g., Supreme Priest-san and Supreme Priest-sama), unless the titles were used to call out to the person themselves, because it doesn't sound awfully good when the person is not part of the conversation themselves.

  
Part 1-specific:  

*   In one instance, I used "but" and "however" one after the other. This is to account for the fact that the original text already used one conjunction (が) like that, but Kirito then decided to throw in a second one (しかし) for good measure.
*   In two instances, Eugeo shortened the auxiliary verb command form "-nasai" (なさい) with the sentence ending particle "yo" (よ) to just "na yo" (なよ). To account for this, I made the sentence that this colloquialism is used in a bit more colloquial than usual (your --> yer and come on --> c'mon).
*   As Kirito is an engrishomaniac with a compulsive desire to use butchered English words to sound cool when decent Japanese words already exist, there is one instance where Kirito talks about wanting to use a butchered English "thank you". I used furigana (ruby) text to make it more clear that he's talking about English words in that instance. Reminder that ruby text works as follows: #_what he meant to say_#(what he actually said)
*   In one instance, Kirito used a colloquial そんで (sonde) instead of それで (sorede). To account for this (lazy) colloquial abbreviation, I shortened "that way" to "t'way".
*   In one scene, two different phrasings (顔をしかめる and 眉を寄せる) that are basically just different ways to say "frowned" were used in the text. So, I translated both instances more literally for a clearer distinction.
*   In the "dear friend of mine" instance, Kirito went grandiose by using the phrase 我が友よ (waga tomo yo), which is a rather overdramatic/archaic way to refer to one's friend.

  
Part 2-specific:  

*   Since I'm a kendoka, I left all stance names as is and used notes to explain what they're like. Note that my explanations are based from personal kendo experience and shinai are two-handed swords, while Kirito usually uses one-handed swords.

  
Part 3-specific:  

*   Bercouli always speaks in a casual masculine tone (uses simple word forms and grammar + adds some masculine sentence ending particles, like "ze" or "zo"), so I tried making him sound a bit extra casual. He does refer to age at one point, but he doesn't use old-timey grammar forms or expressions, so his tone is more like a middle-aged man. As such, I tried not to go overboard with his tone.
*   Purplemelon was written in katakana, rather than hiragana. Probably to indicate that the entire thing is the name for a specific species of melons. So, I used the courier font just like with any kana emphasis cases.
*   The flowers from V10 get referenced in this part. The Japanese name for them is ゼフィリア (zefiria). The fan translation interpreted this as "zephyria", while Yen Press went with "zephilia". However, due to Google Docs prompting me the "zephyria" spelling, I decided to google it and discovered that "zephyria" is the feminine form of the Latin adjective "zephyrus", which means "pertaining to zephyr (west wind/breeze)" based on the ancient Greek god of the west wind, Zephyrus. Seeing as these flowers have the whole gimmick of only blooming in the west empire, I assumed that the gimmick itself was a reference to the Greek god or the west wind, and thus went with the fantranslation version of the name for the flowers.
*   In one instance, Kirito uses 我が (waga, "my"), the possessive case form of the personal pronoun 我 (ware), rather than his usual pronoun 俺 (ore). Ware is a rather formal but dated personal pronoun, usually used in its derivative forms like waga (fancy way to say "my") or wareware (humble way to say "we/us") nowadays. In this case, I just assumed that Kirito either went with the fancy word for emphasis... or to just sound fancy, so I just emphasised the instance with Comic sans font.
*   Japanese loves using English words. However, certain English words are used in different senses than the actual English word is supposed to imply. The English word "image" (イメージ) is more often used in the sense of "visualisation/visualise" (or "imagination", depending on the context) than "image/picture". Normally, I'd just translate it to what makes more sense based on the context, but to make things more complicated, this word at one point references something that Charlotte said back in v10, where it's used more in the sense of "(mental) image"/"imagination" as a synonym for "Incarnation". To maintain the reference... and have the whole reference commentary make sense, I decided to leave the word "image", but also tack on the word "visualise" to get the meaning across better. So don't be surprised when Kirito uses an entire phrase but calls it a word.

  
Part 4-specific:  

*   In the "dear friend" instance, Kirito went semi-grandiose by using the phrase 友よ (tomo yo), which is a rather exaggerated/archaic way to refer to one's friend.

  
Part 5-specific:  

*   Cardinal speaks with archaic grammatical structures and is supposed to sound like an old sage in a young girl's body. So, to account for this, I tried to use archaic and Shakespearean words in her speech, and switched around the word order for it to sound odd. I'm not exactly an expert in archaic English, so please don't expect an accurate depiction of Shakespearean English in my amateurish attempts. I used the following sources for some archaic words: https://learn.lexiconic.net/shakewords.htm; https://www.lexico.com/en/explore/archaic-words.
*   Mithcan is probably derived from the word "mikan" (mandarin orange). I use courier font for it as its name is in katakana.
*   In one instance, Kirito seems to have confused himself and had a brainfart with double negatives. Basically, he kept using different forms of the same word numerous times in the same sentence until he somehow screwed up his grammar and ended up answering a "to be or not to be" question as "to be or not to not be". Or it could have been a typo on Kawahara's part, if he was the one who got confused.

  
Part 6-specific:  

*   The Dark Territory has quite a few synonyms in this story. The official term is ダークテリトリー (Ingurisshu for "Dark Territory"), but it's also called 闇の国 (yami no kuni, "country of darkness") and 暗黒界 (ankokukai, "darkness world"). So, I translated ダークテリトリー as "Dark Territory" with itallics to indicate that it's an English term, 闇の国 as "Land of Darkness" and 暗黒界 as "Darkness Realm".
*   During Alice's conversation with Lipia, both of them start using fancy expressions. I assume that it's supposed to indicate fancy knight etiquette/speech or something. To account for this, I replaced "you"s with "thou"s and used some fancy/archaic words here and there.

  
**Markup explanation:**  

*   _Itallics_ mark text that was written in katakana English (I only highlighted the cases where the author uses both English and actual Japanese equivalents for the same thing, or when I needed to emphasise that an English word is used there).
*   Ｆｕｌｌ－ｗｉｄｔｈ text indicates non-Japanese text that was written in the Latin/English alphabet, rather than katakana. Basically, what you see is exactly how it's written in the raw.
*   Courier font indicates that the original word was written in kana for emphasis (when such a word usually isn't written in kana)
*   Comic sans indicates emphasis added by myself when I needed to stress something to get the meaning across properly.
*   In some instances, the original text used furigana. In cases where the furigana wasn't exact match for the underlying text (usually when the author uses furigana to translate or describe foreign words in Japanese), I maintained the furigana format. Furigana text should be read as follows: #Japanese translation/description of what the term/name means#(What was actually said) (e.g. #shadow fairy race#(Spriggans) - "Spriggans" is what was actually said in text, while "shadow fairy race" is just a brief description of what Spriggans are to help differentiate between the different fairies.
*   In some instances, the author decided to use both English and Japanese equivalents for the exact same word one after the other; in such cases, I marked the English variant in itallics, while the Japanese variant will be emphasised with furigana like this: #Translation into English#(Japanese word) (e.g. #long hair#(chouhatsu) - "long hair" here is a translation of the Japanese word, while "chouhatsu" is a romanisation of the Japanese word in the original text to emphasise that this specific word is in Japanese).
*   In some instances, certain Japanese words or idioms themselves become the topic of attention, but the wordplay/nuance is lost in translation; in this case, the furigana should be understood as such: #Meaning or Japanese word#(What was actually said).
*   Single quotation marks (') are quotes that weren't in the original text but were added for the convenience of English speakers to mark implied quotations. I used it mostly to separate Kirito's internal monologue from his narration, as well as mark sound effects and so on. Actual dialogue lines or quotations (with 「」 punctuation marks) within a narration line are indicated with the usual double (") quotation marks.

"Furyumuguuuuuuuuuuun!"  
  
When I unleashed a shout from the depths of my soul, holding an iron baking tray in my hands, a smile tinged faintly with sorrow appeared on the face of Eugeo, standing in front of me as he said the following.  
  
"Kirito…… wouldn't it be best to just give up on that cake already and make a different one instead? The tea party is the day after tomorrow, you know, and if you don't manage to bring a new cake to it, both of us will be turned into plates by the Supreme Priest, you know?  
  
"Funuuuun……"  
  
After responding in an enigmatic language, I looked down at the object on the baking tray I had just taken out of the _oven_.  
  
Twenty centimetres-wide, five centimetres-tall, and cylindrical. The colour: cinder-like jet-black. Actually, not ‘like’. The black colour was the result of the object being scorched, so it was a prime example of cinders.  
  
"……Well, you know…… The ingredients for it are just wheat flour and sugar, then some eggs, _cheese_, _cream_, and some Siral fruit juice. All we had to do was mix them all together and bake the thing, so there's no reason for it to be scorched black like this in a mere minute, is there."  
  
When I returned to speaking in the Human Empire language as I said this, Eugeo nodded with an expression that made it clear it didn't make sense to him either.  
  
"Well…… I myself do think that it's strange. It's even more enigmatic that the cake is still utterly half-baked if you take it out of the _oven_ at the fifty-nine second mark."  
  
"You said it. What the heck happens between the fifty-nine and sixty second mark……"  
  
Shaking my head to the sides, I gazed at the large _oven_.  
  
The place we were occupying at the moment was a corner of the Grand Kitchen on the tenth floor of the Central Cathedral. Even this gigantic _kitchen_, filled with the bustling orchestra of kitchen knives, pots and frying pans during the day, had fallen completely silent by now. That was because it was now past ten at night, so the head chef and all his cooks had long since returned to their rooms.  
  
Honestly, I am quite sleepy myself and the same probably applies to Eugeo as well. During the day, we were mercilessly worked to the bone from nine till five by high-ranked knight Scheta-sensei; completely exhausted, we took up working on our cake prototype, thus by ten o'clock, the weight of our eyelids is reaching our limits. Till yesterday, this would be the point where we'd finish up our work, but we can't exactly do so tonight until we have achieved some progress. Just like Eugeo mentioned moments ago, if we don't manage to complete a new cake by noon on the rest day two days away, we would be punished by Her Holy Eminence, the Supreme Priest _Administrator_. I don't really think we'd actually be turned into plates as she had declared…… wait, I should probably assume that it's quite plausible for that to happen.  
  
Heaving a sigh, I placed the pitch-black lump that should have become a _baked cheesecake_ onto the kitchen counter. Due to the faintest shock, the fragile lump crumbled, losing all of its Life before disappearing.  
  
The reason why Eugeo and I are endeavouring to bake cakes in the Axiom Church's Central Cathedral. That's because the ruler of the Human Empire, _Administrator_, promised us that she would continue her talks with the other Supreme Priest, Cardinal, as long as we can make new cakes for them.  
  
Both _Administrator_'s and Cardinal's ultimate goal is to protect the Human Empire from the near-at-hand invasion by the army of darkness. There's no way that the two going into a deathmatch against each other would be a good idea, thus we have to ensure by any means that the rest day tea party continues on, but at this rate, it's going to end in disaster on just the second instance.  
  
"……Say, Kirito. Wouldn't it be best to just give up on this _cheesecake_ thingie and make a simpler cake instead?"  
  
Staring down at the now-empty baking tray, I feebly responded to Eugeo's words.  
  
  
"A _cheesecake_ is supposed to be about as simple, if not even simpler, to make than the _shortcake_ we baked last week. There's no need to cover it in _cream_, nor is it necessary to adorn it with strawberries……"  
  
"Eeeh? Wouldn't that make its appearance a bit too basic……? How should I put it… sounds like it would just be a flat bun or something……"  
  
"Well…… it might be more basic in appearance than a _shortcake_, but that's the good part about it. The surface baked to a goldish-brown, contrasted by the light yellow of its insides is just……"  
  
My own words made me recall the taste of a _baked cheesecake_ that I haven't tasted in three years by now, prompting me to gulp down the saliva in my mouth. It's not like I’m cray-cray-crazy for sweets, but out of all cakes out there, a rich _cheesecake_ is my favourite one. Honestly, the fact that I wanted to eat one myself may have had a little sway in my choice to have this cake in particular for this week's tea party's menu. However, it can't be that my self-interest has led to the cake always ending up scorched-black.  
  
"……Is there really no way for us to look inside the _oven_ while it's heating……"  
  
When I muttered this, my partner shrugged his shoulders as if to say, 'what stupid thing are you thinking of now'.  
  
"The only way to look inside is to open the door. But if you do open it, the heat will escape, causing a cooking failure, you know."  
  
"Right indeed……"  
  
I had to nod at that.  
  
If this were an _oven_ from the real world, you wouldn't end up with a cooking failure just because you opened the door a little bit while it's heating to check on how well the dish has cooked; however, excessively strict requirements are imposed in Underworld. As Eugeo mentioned just now, if we were to open the _oven_'s door before it's done cooking, the temperature inside will instantly drop and even if we heated it again, the cake would never reach its completed state. In other words, the cooks in this world have the following ironclad rule about opening the _oven_: 《Don't open the door even if you hear a baby crying inside》.  
  
In fact, we were prepared to waste one cake's worth of ingredients and attempted to open the door the moment we reached the fifty-nine second mark, but the _cheesecake_ was still basically raw at that point. Yet when we open it at the sixty second mark, it's already scorched-black. In order to check on what exactly happens in that single second, it doesn't seem like we have any other choice but to install a _glass_ window into the _oven_ door; however, _glass_ production and processing in Underworld is a cutting edge industry that requires proficient craftsmen and high-grade equipment, thus even us Integrity Knight apprentices can't exactly just waltz up to a Centoria _glass_ workshop all of a sudden and tell the craftsmen, "Make us a small window for an _oven_ door!".  
  
In that case, we could just go ＤＩＹ… or at least I'd like to do so, but we don't exactly have the time to start learning the art of _glass_ production right now, nor do we even know what exactly _glass_ is made from in this world, for starters. If we considered what other transparent materials we could use instead, I can't think of anything other than crystals and diamonds, but those are bound to be even more valuable commodities than _gla_……  
  
"Oh, wait, hold on."  
  
As I muttered this, I was met with a questioning look from Eugeo. After replying to him with a broad grin, I raised my right hand and began chanting a rite.  
  
"_System call. Generate crystalline element_."  
  
The small dot of light produced on my fingertip was one of the eight _elements_, the 《crystal element》. Since it was an element with even fewer uses than the steel and darkness elements, we didn't really use it much even during our lessons at the Swordcraft Academy, but now it was our last hope.  
  
"_Form element, plate shape_."  
  
As I continued the chant, the dot of light turned into the shape of a thin plate via my _command_ and _imagination_, before materialising with a faint "kin" sound. What fell into my palm was a twenty centimetre square _glass_ plate, five millimetres in thickness.  
  
"……What exactly do you plan to do with it?"  
  
As my partner looked at me with an even more distrustful expression, I gave him a slight _wink_ and closed the _valves_ on the _oven_'s sides with one hand, before bringing the _glass_ plate to the centre of the Grand Kitchen.  
  
There, a pure white marble——the same material that the Cathedral's outer wall was made of—— hemisphere was enshrined. Steel _pipes_ extended in eight directions from the bottom part of the likely one-and-a-half-metre-wide hemisphere; a short tube was furnished at its top, red flames spouting out of it every now and then.  
  
This hemisphere was a Divine Object known as the 《Kiln of Eternal Flames》[^1], created by Supreme Priest _Administrator_ with her super high-ranked _system control_ authority; sealed inside of it were heat elements that never went out. Known as the most fastidious of all the elements, heat elements would gradually release their heat just by being kept in the air, until they eventually disappeared; or if they touched any sort of object, they would just heat it up that very moment and, likewise, disappear, thus I don't have the slightest idea on how the heck was anyone able to contain them in a marble kiln.  
  
The _pipes_ extending from the kiln connected to the stoves and _ovens_ throughout the Kitchen, providing them all with intense heat for cooking. Right now, the _valves_ at the end of the _pipes_ were all closed, which meant that the energy from the heat elements was now escaping little by little through the tube at the top of the kiln.  
  
Standing slightly on tiptoes, I held the _glass_ plate in my right hand over the faintly-red flame.  
  
"Oi, Kirito, what are you……"  
  
"An experiment, just an experiment. Oh, just watch."  
  
Responding thus to Eugeo after he spoke out in concern, I focused on the _glass_ plate.  
  
Utensils made out of steel and crystal elements on the whole have low Life values. Even this _glass_ plate would probably easily break if I were to apply some force while holding it between my hands, but what about its heat resistance? Even if it had poor physical strength, its resistance against intense heat and cold…… in other words, its resistance against elemental _damage_ was a different story altogether.  
  
Warmed by the flame, the central portion of the _glass_ began to shine red before long; but however it had yet to break. Twenty seconds, thirty seconds, forty seconds…………  
  
"Uo-hoooot!"  
  
Feeling an intense sensation of heat at my fingertips all of a sudden, I threw away the _glass_ plate.  
  
"Towah!"  
  
The reason why even Eugeo let out a scream was because the red-hot _glass_ plate flew right next to his feet. The _glass_ crumbled to pieces near my partner's feet after he had _jumped_ away. However, I believe that it most likely wasn't broken by the heat, but rather by the shock of the crash. If I hadn't thrown it away, it seems it would have lasted for over sixty seconds.  
  
"Oh geez, what the heck are you doing here……"  
  
Saying thus with an appalled expression, Eugeo quickly created a freezing element, turning it into a lump of ice.  
  
"Here, cool off yer fingers."  
  
"Thanks."  
  
This would be a moment where I'd prefer to say #_thank you_#(sankyuu), but that's a _word_ that doesn't exist in this world, thus I expressed my gratitude in the Human Empire language, before taking the ice. With my heated fingertips rapidly cooled off, the throbbing pain practically disappeared entirely.  
  
"So, how did your experiment go?"  
  
"Succeeded, probably."  
  
At that answer, a sceptical expression once again found its way on Eugeo's face.  
  
"'Probably', you say…… what the heck are you planning to do with that plate?"  
  
"I'll leave you in suspense, so you'd have something to look forward to seeing. Though, it really is getting late today, so let's save it for tomorrow."  
  
"Is that really a good idea? The tea party is already taking place the day after tomorrow."  
  
"That means we still have one more day, right."  
  
Answering thus, I slapped my partner on his back. On the inside, I was greatly panicking, but since I was the one who dragged Eugeo into the Cake Plan, I didn't want my unease to show on my face at the very least.  
  
"Tomorrow, let's try explaining our circumstances to Scheta-sensei and ask her to allow us to wrap up our training in the morning. T'way, if we focused on the job starting from when we finished our grub for lunch and worked until ten at night, I'm sure we'd be able to resolve even the scorching issue."  
  
"Hmm, I wonder if the Scheta-sensei would actually agree to giving us half a day off……"  
  
"Even Sensei doesn't want to see me and you being turned into plates…… or at least I think she wouldn't. Besides, don't you think that person is somehow similar to Azurica-sensei from the Swordcraft Academy?"  
  
"Well…… I do feel that the aura they give off is just the slightest bit similar, but what of it?"  
  
"Azurica-sensei has overlooked me breaking the curfew on numerous occasions, so Scheta-sensei must actually be a nice person inside as well, I'm sure of it."  
  
At my wishful thinking, Eugeo just slightly shook his head.  
  
  
Having finished up our experiment at the Grand Kitchen and arrived at our room on the twenty-eighth floor following a lot of stair climbing, Eugeo and I heaved a sigh just as we both collapsed onto the sofa.  
  
At this point, I would love to head to the Grand Bath on the ninetieth floor to recover from all that physical and mental labour, but the elevating disk doesn't operate at this late hour. I didn't have the energy to climb another sixty-two _floors_ worth of stairs, thus I considered going to the shared bathroom on this floor——though, seeing as we are the only apprentice knights living on the twenty-eighth floor at the moment, it's practically our exclusive bathroom——but I can't be bothered to get up for that.  
  
'I'll just take a bath in the morning…… actually, I feel too languid to even go to my own bed……', as I was overwhelmed by such slovenly thoughts, it only took moments for my eyelids to drop down.  
  
"Oi, Kirito, you'll catch a cold if you fall asleep there."  
  
With my right shoulder violently shaken by Eugeo, I somehow managed to just half-open my right eye.  
  
"It's all good, I am an Integrity Knight, even if only an apprentice, so I won't catch a cold by simply falling asleep on the sofa."  
  
"What does being a knight have to do with that!"  
  
Asserting thus with an appalled expression, Eugeo stood up straight, changing his shaking attack into a pulling attack.  
  
"C'mon, get up before you fall asleep. Have you forgotten the time you dozed off at a sheep farm in the middle of the winter when you were a child and caught a cold because of it?"  
  
"Yeah yeah……"  
  
The moment I reluctantly stood up, my drowsiness was blown away as if it had never even been there. Staring fixedly at my partner's face head-on, I enquired in a hoarse voice.  
  
"……Oi, Eugeo, what did you just……"  
  
"Ah……"  
  
Eyes bulged open, Eugeo instantly screwed up his face, lifting his right hand to touch his brow.  
  
"……There it goes again. Just like the time you gave me a cake to celebrate my birthday, Kirito……I felt as if we had been together ever since we were little……"  
  
"…………"  
  
Having no idea how to respond to that, I contracted my brow as well.  
  
Me having spent my childhood with Eugeo at Rulid Village can't have actually happened. The first time I _dived_ into 《this _version_ of Underworld》 was three years ago; by that point, Eugeo was already a seventeen-year-old young man. When we first met under the _Gigas Cedar_, Eugeo recognised neither my face, nor my name and vice versa.  
  
However, I, too, experienced the phenomenon that contradicts this fact five days ago——on Eugeo's birthday. When he saw my _shortcake present_ for him, Eugeo muttered, "I haven't been this happy with a present since that time you made me a wooden sword from silveroak when we were kids.", and I replied, "I've had quite some trouble with that sword too, but it was easier since Alice made the scabbard for it."  
  
At that moment, I vividly recalled. How I had given Eugeo a wooden sword, which I had made from a silveroak branch over a period of two months of work at a secret vacant plot in the eastern forest, as a _present_ to celebrate his birthday. That was his tenth or eleventh birthday…… what's more, I also remember how Alice had made a scabbard for the wooden sword at the same place as well……  
  
Humans are living beings who sometimes create fake memories, yet I can't find any way to explain why that scene felt so much _real_istic, nor how I'm sharing the same memory with Eugeo. In the end, our only way to find out the whole truth is to ask the people from Rath who had created Underworld and are now managing it. When I once again looked at Amayori, I saw that Eugeo, having finally got used to flying, was now having some sort of conversation with Alice. I couldn't hear what they were talking about, but it seemed he was doing his best as usual. Now that I think back upon it, I feel that it took him less time to break the ice with his valet Tiese than it took me to break the ice with Ronye, thus having contact with girls may, unexpectedly, not be one of his weak points.  
  
However——  
  
As I contemplated earlier, Eugeo's endeavours to close the distance between him and Knight Alice may one day end up entirely wasted.  
  
"……_Synthesising_, huh……"  
  
The moment I muttered that word unconsciously, a jolt ran through _Administrator_'s back in the front seat. Before I even had the time to think "Oh shit", the Supreme Priest turned around, her silver eyes coldly piercing my eyes.  
  
"……So shrimpie told you something she shouldn't have, I see. So then, how much do you know about the _Synthesis_ Ritual?"  
  
"Ehm…… umm……"  
  
I gulped, but now that it's come to this, my only choice was to answer truthfully.  
  
"……Taking out a 《memory fragment》 from the knights, sealing their memories before becoming knights and implanting a 《_Piety Module_》 in their place to enforce loyalty to the Axiom Church and the Esteemed Supreme Priest…… that's what constitutes a 《_Synthesis_ Ritual》 , I was told."  
  
"That's about right…… So? What do you want to say to me, Kirito _Synthesis Thirty-three_?"  
  
"Eh…...mm…… if I am honest, you wouldn't turn me into a plate or a carrot, would you……?"  
  
"Of course not, probably."  
  
"Th-then in that case, allow me to say it…… setting aside the pros and cons of _synthesising_, I do believe that the 《Integrity Knights have been summoned from the Celestial World》 thing is a bit nonsensical……"  
  
When I mustered the courage to say that, _Administrator_ once again turned around and looked at me with an even more chilly glare.  
  
"What do you mean, nonsensical?"  
  
"Well, ya know, basically all of the knights are actually either the winners of the Four Empires Unification Tournament like us, or criminals that violated the Taboo Index, is it not so? So, you take them and seal their memories by _synthesising_ them, and then give them new memories that they've been summoned from the Celestial World…… Ain't that going to mean that every time you _synthesise_ a single person, there'd be a need to adjust the memories of all the knights that had contact with this knight, ma'am? For example, if Eugeo and I were to be _synthesised_ and become knights proper, you would need to rewrite the memories of Alice and Scheta-sensei while you're at it or questions would arise, would you not……?"  
  
"They do arise. Actually, since that's exactly what I'm doing, it happens every time."  
  
_Administrator_ responded in a somewhat sulky voice. Upon turning back to the front, she thrust up her fists into the sky all of a sudden, before shouting the following.  
  
"It's a pain in the ass; simply absurd! But what choice do I have; that's the story I went with at the beginning, after all! As if I can just say 'Oh, you guys were actually people from the Human Empire all along' now of all times!"  
  
"Th…… then, how about you at the very least give up on it hereafter; on _synthesising_, I mean, ma'am? If you make us and the other knights 'Appointed Knights' rather than 'Summoned Knights', you would no longer need to adjust the memories of all the knights each time, woul-……."  
  
"Hey, you're just saying all that because you don't want to be _synthesised_ yourself. I'll have you know that the 《_Synthesis_》 that is your _middle name_ implies a person who's been _synthesised_, you hear. I should have told you this when we first met; 'Do your best so that those names come true', I said."  
  
"Well, ya know, if you ask me whether I want to be synthesised, or whether I don't want to be not synthesised, I would choose 'I don't want to be synthesised'."  
  
"In that case, there's only one way to achieve that. Just like you had me form a truce with Cardinal, find a way to do something about the _Dark Territory_ invasion. The Integrity Knights exist for two reasons…… the first is to kill Cardinal, the other is to repel the forces of darkness. If both of these reasons ceased to exist, I'd have no need to increase the number of knights anymore. Even I feel a few regrets about it; if I were to _synthesise_ you, I'd probably lose your ability to make all those cakes too, after all."  
  
Once she declared all of this in one go while facing forward, _Administrator_ once again reclined on the backrest.  
  
"……I'm going to sleep for awhile. Wake me up when we reach Mount Shunca."  
  
——'Um, I don't exactly know what that mountain looks like though, ma'am.'  
  
Was what I was about to say, but I closed my mouth just as I opened it. Instead, I slackened my hold of the reins a bit, entrusting Yukiori with figuring out the destination.  
  
  

6
-----

  
Turns out there was no need to keep my eyes peeled for Mount Shunca. After all, it was a mass of rocks that shot up to seven or eight hundred metres smack dab in the middle of a flatland, sticking out like a sore thumb on the same level as the Tokyo _Skytree_.  
  
Convinced that this had to be our destination for sure, I gently shook _Administrator_ awake. Having taken back the reins from me, the Supreme Priest had Yukiori ascend along the rock face and dive straight through a gigantic _gate_ constructed at its peak without any hesitation.  
  
The same peculiar sensation I had when I was _teleported_ to the Great Library Room from the rose garden assailed me again, a rainbow light engulfing my vision; when I subconsciously opened my closed eyelids the next moment, I found that we were no longer at Mount Shunca. Standing in our way in front of us was a gigantic mountain mass that hadn't been there just seconds ago. The pure white mountain range that seemed to pierce the heavens was undoubtedly the 《End Mountains》 that encircled the Human Empire, the same ones that I had seen up-close from Rulid Village. As the time now was past three o'clock in the afternoon, the light from now-more-yellowish Solus caused the ridgeline to sparkle.  
  
Though, there was one single difference from the mountain range in the north. A deep ravine was carved out of the mountain surface straight ahead of us as if a gigantic god had sawed it out, dividing the mountain range that was two thousand or so metres higher than the surrounding area into two.  
  
Upon sending a fleeting glance to Alice behind us, the Supreme Priest had Yukiori plunge straight into the ravine. The moment sunlight was obstructed by the perpendicular cliff, a chill engulfed my entire body. Even I, as inept in the Arts as I was, have started getting the hang of feeling the state of the local spatial Sacred Power with my skin recently; yet, the supply of Sacred Power in this ravine was surprisingly low. Although some _resources_ themselves had accumulated there over the course of many, many years, it'd probably take a humongous amount of time for it to charge up again if all of them were used up.  
  
The two dragons slowly advanced through the hundred-or-so-metre-wide ravine. As the cliffs at our sides grew taller, our vicinity rapidly grew dimmer. Yet, what appeared tens of seconds later gave off such a prominent presence even within the darkness that it was bizarre.  
  
An unfathomably gigantic——gate. Scale-wise, it rivaled the Central Cathedral.  
  
The gray gate jutting out from the bottom of the ravine probably extended to a thousand metre height. I couldn't even tell whether it was made of stone or metal. It was so exceedingly big that it threw my perception of distance out of whack and I shrank into myself in fear that we'd end up crashing into it if we got any closer, yet the Supreme Priest continued having Yukiori move on nevertheless.  
  
When her dainty hands finally pulled on the reins, my entire field of vision was covered up entirely by the gray structure. Even when I raised my head, I couldn't see through to its top because of a thin haze that covered it.  
  
"So this…… is the 《Great Eastern Gate》……"  
  
When I muttered this in a hoarse voice, _Administrator_ responded without hiding her irritation.  
  
"That's right. The curtain that protects the Human Empire, yet will cause a war."  
  
"A curtain……? It looks far too sturdy for what you cal-……"  
  
"Take a good look at its surface."  
  
Upon being told this, I strained my eyes towards the great gate towering in the darkness. That very moment, I let out a small gulp.  
  
Numerous cracks were running like a mesh along the gray surface. As I stared at it dumbfounded, one of the cracks spread out with a loud noise, making contact with another crack. Small fragments poured down from a _dumper truck_\-sized lump that was encircled by a triangular crack; even now it seemed like it could fall off the gate itself at any moment.  
  
Just then, _Administrator_ extended her right hand. Although you normally can't possibly make more than five _elements_ with one hand, she managed to create about twenty metal elements on her hand in one go, and without any incantation to boot. When the gray dots of light flew off and soaked into the crack, the lump that was about to fall off stopped vibrating.  
  
"……Is that how… you continued mending this hugeass gate, ma'am? All this time ever since last summer……?"  
  
"That's right. Honestly, it's like trying to cool off a hot stone with a few drops of water…… The decline of the Great Gate's Life is so fast that I can't possibly keep up with it by simply adhering metal elements to it, yet if I were to use a large scale rite, I'd end up exhausting all the Sacred Power in this ravine. Even delaying the process like this is only going to work for another half a year at best."  
  
"…………"  
  
I could only gnaw at my lips.  
  
The Supreme Priest _Administrator_ had mentioned this during last week's tea party. That the reason why she had suspended her Mechanical Sword Soldier Project whatsit and continued mending the Great Eastern Gate… was because she bet on the potential that I had shown with Eugeo. Her "Find a way to do something about the _Dark Territory_ invasion" remark wasn't just a mere joke. The Supreme Priest was seriously thinking that we might possibly find a way to save the world. Just like how we created the _shortcake_ or the _cheesecake_.  
  
"……What if… this gate were to break down…… what would happen then……?"  
  
"I'll show you."  
  
Upon muttering this, the Supreme Priest pulled Yukiori's reins. The silver dragon who didn't lose its gleam even in this darkness cried out a short "Kururu", before flapping its elegant wings. Its large body rose up perpendicularly as if it were a ＶＴＯＬ aircraft. Slightly below, Alice's Amayori followed suit.  
  
As I looked at the gray wall flowing in front of me, I suddenly felt as if I spotted some kind of text in the Latin _alphabet_ carved into it. However, I didn't have enough time to read it before it disappeared below us, giving way to the top of the Great Gate. I do believe that it'd be impossible for both man and beast to climb all the way up here, but it seemed that numerous sharp steel barbs were set up at the upper edge. About ten metres above them, _Administrator_ had her dragon hover in place.  
  
"…………"  
  
Without a single word, I fixed my gaze beyond the gate——at the desolate scenery of the _Dark Territory_ that I actually laid eyes on for the first time.  
  
I can see why it's called the Land of Darkness. The ground was as black as coal ash; the sparsely growing trees were twisted as if in agony; not a single watering hole could be found—— and the sky was as red as blood, even though there should have still been some time till the evening. Compared to this spectacle, even GGO's wasteland maps could still be regarded as being filled with natural life.  
  
The phrase 'world of death' crossed my mind; however, I was forced to immediately revise that notion. Far beyond the short ravine that extended to the opposite side of the Great Gate, there were several lights swaying on a plateau that was slightly higher than the surrounding ground. Bonfires…… someone had set up camp over there.  
  
"……What are those fires for……?"  
  
"The _Dark Territory_'s advance forces, obviously. They're observing how this gate crumbles away bit by bit. Once its collapse is at hand, the entire flatland you see there will probably be filled up entirely by the grand army of the five races of the Darkness Realm."  
  
"Five races…… as I recall, those were _goblins, orcs, ogres, giants_, and……"  
  
"Humans. People of the Dark Realm."  
  
As _Administrator_ answered as quietly as a mutter, her voice seemed to have such a cold ring to it that it gave me the shivers. Is she angry……? Towards the army of darkness……? Or perhaps……  
  
At that moment.  
  
"Esteemed Supreme Priest! Something is coming!"  
  
Alice's voice rang out sharply. When I strained my eyes in panic, I saw several black dots coming towards us in the deep red sky. Bit by bit they became bigger, finally taking form. Black wings and long tails……  
  
"D-dragons? The _Dark Territory_ has dragon knights too?"  
  
"Of course they do. They're dark knights. The main force of the army of darkness, who have crossed swords with the Integrity Knights for hundreds of years. If they've noticed my presence and are now coming to take my head, they sure underestimate me, huh."  
  
As the Supreme Priest declared this, Alice's shout overlapped with her words.  
  
"I shall make short work of them, thus I would like to humbly ask you to stand back, Supreme Priest-sama!"  
  
As Alice was about to have Amayori charge ahead with Eugeo still riding on its back the moment she shouted this, _Administrator_ quickly thrust out her right hand to stop her.  
  
"That will not be necessary, Alice-chan. I have to make an example here by eradicating them in a single attack so great that it causes the advance troops behind them to scurry away as well, letting them know what befalls those who dare approach the Human Empire."  
  
Her right hand moved to her left hip, slowly unsheathing a rapier. She turned the _mirror-silver_ blade, sparkling like a mirror, towards the dragons closing in on us at high speed.  
  
The dragons closing in on us numbered four. When I strained my eyes, it seemed that there was indeed a human riding atop the dragon in the vanguard. So that's a dark knight——that is, a human living in the _Dark Territory_, huh. While I did have an interest in finding out what kind of beings they were exactly, it didn't seem I'd get the chance to get a close look at them.  
  
_Administrator_'s rapier bellowed, 'Buuuun……', as it was wreathed in white light. While it wasn't all that flashy as a phenomenon, I could feel with my bare skin just how tremendous the _energy_ contained within the sword was. The light gathered at the tip of the rapier, condensing and letting out a shrill, high-frequency noise. If this were to be fired out, it would cause the evaporation of both the four approaching dragons and their riders without even leaving a corpse behi-……  
  
"…………!?"  
  
That instant, my eyes shot up wide as I shouted out reflexively.  
  
"H-hold it! _Stop, stop_!"  
  
It's unclear whether my English there got through to her, but the Supreme Priest jolted her rapier upwards, before giving me a stern look.  
  
"What exactly is your problem, _Thirty-three_?"  
  
"I would like to ask you to take a closer look; only the one in the lead is an actual dragon, while the three in the back look somehow different!"  
  
"Huh?"  
  
Frowning, _Administrator_ turned her gaze to the four approaching silhouettes and immediately said the following.  
  
"Oh my, you're right. The three in the back appear to be large _minions_. Though, that changes nothing about them being enemies."  
  
Cardinal had spoken of the term '_minion_' during last week's tea party. It seems that they were likewise winged creatures, but their necks weren't as long as those of dragons, while their taloned limbs just dangled down languidly. They looked more like _gargoyles_ than _dragons_.  
  
However, that wasn't the problem.  
  
"Wait, look, doesn't it seem like the dragon in the lead is being chased, ma'am?"  
  
"Hmn? Mnn, that may be true, but like I care. I'm going to kill them in the end anyways, after all."  
  
"W-wait wait wait, aren't you interested in why they're being chased though, ma'am?"  
  
"They probably just snagged someone's snack or something."  
  
"That's not the impression I'm getting here, ma'am. Can't we save the killing for after we find out the reason behind what's going on, ma'am?"  
  
"You sure are a pain in the ass……"  
  
Even as she continued giving the biggest frown she could, _Administrator_ still made a slight change to the angle of her rapier.  
  
"Then I'll only take down the _minions_ in the back."  
  
"Y-yes please"  
  
"Eih"  
  
With her not-all-that-pumped shout, a pure white _laser_ was discharged from the tip of the rapier. It instantly split into three, forming large arcs before piercing the winged monsters chasing the black dragon.  
  
'Pigyaaaa!', a faint scream could be heard. The three monsters called _minions_ fell down one after another, scattering black blood in the sky in their wake, until they disappeared from our sight.  
  
The pursued dragon's _speed_ went down. It wobbled in the sky, looking like it could crash down at any moment. It seemed that the rider atop the saddle was arduously trying to rein in the dragon, but they just couldn't get it to stabilise its flight.  
  
"Sigh…… if that dark knight comes pouncing on us after all this, be sure to take responsibility for it and defeat them yourself."  
  
Upon giving this order to me, _Administrator_ once again hoisted her rapier.  
  
Light element _energy_ was what shot out of it this time as well. Yet rather than producing another super-powered _laser_, it instead formed a _ribbon_\-shaped _mist_. The white _ribbons_ gently flowed through the sky, enveloping the black dragon that had closed in by another two hundred metres by then.  
  
Having probably had its Life healed up by the Supreme Priest's healing Arts, the dragon's wing flaps regained their strength. It increased its _speed_, heading straight for us. I could already make out the details of the knight riding on its back. Their entire body was enveloped in a purplish-black helmet and armour and I couldn't see their face.  
  
Alice's Amayori flew in front of Yukiori. 'Jarin', with a noise the girl pulled out her golden sword. Despite witnessing its radiance, the dark knight did not stop their advance.  
  
Eventually, once the black dragon closed in to a point right in front of us on the other side of the Great Eastern Gate, it finally switched over to hovering mode. As if trying to indicate they had no intention to fight, the knight in its saddle hoisted their open right arm, before shouting.  
  
"Pray tell me, do I have the pleasure of happening upon an Integrity Knight of the Axiom Church!"  
  
At that voice that sounded muffled due to the helmet, Alice responded sonorously.  
  
"Indeed thou have! For what purpose do thou approach this Great Gate, guarded by us Integrity Knights!"  
  
Just then the dark knight placed their hands on their helmet and pulled it off their head without hesitation. Her long, purplish-black hair streamed out. A woman……?  
  
As I gazed in wondernment, the knight's coquettish _husky voice_ rang out.  
  
"I am the eleventh-ranked Dark Knight, one who is known as Lipia Zancale! I have come to this land bearing a decree from my lord! I would like to seek an audience with the Commander of the Integrity Knights!"
