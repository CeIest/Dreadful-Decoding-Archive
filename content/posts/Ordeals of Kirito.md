Ordeals of Kirito (キリトの受難, Kirito no Junan) is an original SAO story featured in the [Accel World + Sword Art Online Drama CD](http://swordartonline.wikia.com/wiki/Accel_World_%2B_Sword_Art_Online_Drama_CD). This story is available in audio format, but the disc also came with a script. Since I was translating from the script, you'll also find the narration translated, because some of the things mentioned in it weren't audible in the audio (and some of the remarks were funny).  
  
If anyone spots any mistakes, feel free to point them out in the comments.  
  
_Note: I'm not really sure what the big ol' M next to the names is supposed to stand for, but it basically means that the following line isn't heard by other characters._  


_2025 Note: The original YouTube uploader of the subtitled videos has deleted their channel, the videos are now unavailable._
